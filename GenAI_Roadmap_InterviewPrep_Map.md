**The interview-prep track of the AI Engineering Roadmap 2026.** Every episode of the [Concepts & Theory track](../AI_Concepts_and_Theory/README.md) has a matching interview-prep set — real questions, real answers, calibrated to what MAANG-level AI Engineer interviews actually test.

Unlike the theory track (one repo per episode), the interview prep lives in a **single repo, organized week-by-week**, where each week maps to an episode.

## What Each Week's Prep Contains

Consistent structure across every week, so you always know where to look:

- **README** — the week's map: what the interview tests and how to navigate the questions.
- **Deep Conceptual** — the "explain and defend" questions, with flagship long-form model answers.
- **Technical Coding** — implementation questions with full runnable solutions.
- **System Design** — the architecture questions, calibrated junior through staff-plus.
- **Behavioral Scenarios** — the "tell me about a time" and judgment questions specific to AI work.
- **Terminology Differentiation** — the "what's the difference between X and Y" questions that reveal depth.

## How To Use Both Tracks Together

The intended loop for each episode:
1. **Read the [concepts/theory](../AI_Concepts_and_Theory/README.md) episode** — build the real understanding.
2. **Work the matching interview-prep week** — pressure-test that understanding against what interviews demand.
3. **Move to the next episode** only when the self-check passes.

Theory without prep leaves you able to build but not to explain. Prep without theory leaves you able to recite but not to reason. Together they're the full path from learner to hired.
